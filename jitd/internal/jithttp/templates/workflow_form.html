Okta groupsAdds the requester to these Okta groups until the access ends.
How it works

An approval adds the requester's Okta user (matched by login, then by email) to each group, and jitd removes them when the access ends. Okta memberships have no expiry of their own, so removal needs jitd running; a group the person was already in stays as it was. Only Okta-mastered groups can be used.

{{end}} {{if $hasGoogle}}
Google groupsAdds the requester to these Google groups until the access ends.
How it works, and what to use it for

An approval adds the requester to each group, under their Tailscale login as the email address, and jitd removes them when the access ends. Google memberships have no expiry here, so removal needs jitd running; someone who was in the group already stays as they were. Use this for what Google gates by group: Google Cloud roles, apps signed in to through Google, shared drives. Not for opening Tailscale access: Tailscale reads Google groups only every 40 minutes, so the access would start late and end late. Use Tailscale access for that.

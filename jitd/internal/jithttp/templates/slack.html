Connect Slack

  1. Create the Slack app. This opens Slack with the app filled in: its name, the bot, Socket Mode, the /jit command and the permissions jitd needs. Pick your workspace and click Create.
    Create the Slack app
  2. Install it. On the app's page, open Install App, click Install to your workspace and allow it. If it says Request to Install, a workspace admin has to approve the app first. Then open OAuth & Permissions and copy the Bot User OAuth Token; it starts with xoxb-.
  3. Make an app-level token. Under Basic Information → App-Level Tokens, click Generate Token and Scopes, name it jitd, add the connections:write scope and copy the token; it starts with xapp-.
  4. Paste both here.

The tokens are kept in jitd's database and are never shown again, here or in the API. To keep them out of the database, put SLACK_BOT_TOKEN and SLACK_APP_TOKEN (and SLACK_ALLOWED_DOMAINS) in {{$st.EnvFile}} instead and restart jitd.

How it works

  • Each approval flow can post new requests to a channel, and send each approver a direct message. The message updates itself as the request is approved, declined, expires or is revoked.
  • Approve and Decline in Slack run the same checks as the browser: who may approve, no self-approval unless the flow allows it, how many approvals it needs. Revoking stays in the browser.
  • In the browser, Tailscale says who you are. In Slack, jitd goes by your Slack email: you must be a full member of this workspace (no guests, no one from a shared channel's other company) with a confirmed email in an allowed domain, that email must be your Tailscale login, and that login must own a device on the tailnet. Decisions made this way are marked via Slack on the request, with the Slack account that made them.
  • For a flow that should only be decided in the browser, turn on Browser only on its page: its messages then carry no buttons.
  • /jit in Slack lists what is waiting for you and your own requests. /jit request asks for access: pick a flow and one of your own devices (Tailscale lists them by your login).
